Oninus Lactis NG 2.2

The Papyrus scripts, the plugin, the squirt meshes and the textures come from Oninus
Lactis by Orbus Ninus and Oninus Lactis NG 2.1, under their terms: free to use and
build upon with attribution, no paid distribution.

OninusLactis.dll and its source (src\, tools\) are by crajjjj, GNU General Public
License version 3; see LICENSE (OninusLactis-LICENSE.txt in the mod archive).

The DLL is built from Lacrima (https://github.com/crajjjj/lacrima, GPL-3.0), which
contains code adapted from OStrands by LeonVanWinkle (Saint), also GPL-3.0, taken
from OStrands 0.8.0 (https://gitlab.com/LilRicefield/ostrands):

- src/fluid/*: the position-based fluid solver, skin lookup grid, clusters and
  density-field surface, as adapted in Lacrima (a simulation frame that moves with a
  bone, fluid at rest sleeps, front-face skin contact).
- src/GeometryMath.h: the rope frame transport.
- src/Body.cpp: the skin wrap (vertex decoding, skinning, the region test), widened
  from Lacrima's face to every skinned mesh on the torso, with the nipple search
  added.
- src/Milk.cpp: Lacrima's Tears.cpp (the fluid rendering: blobs, ropes, drops; the
  emission, sleep and trail logic), fed from the nipples, with one simulation frame
  per breast bone. Its running-mascara code is still in the file and idle.
- src/Hooks.cpp: the input-event frame driver (the fallback since 2.3.4).
- tools/nifkit.py and tools/make_milk.py: the NIF and DDS writers.

vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/SKSEMenuFramework.h is the client header of SKSE Menu
Framework, GPL-3.0; see vendor/SKSEMenuFramework/README.txt.
